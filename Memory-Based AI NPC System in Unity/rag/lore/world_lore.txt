Long ago, before the villages were united, Eldoria was only scattered settlements fighting to survive. Bandits controlled the trade roads, crops failed often, and trust between villages was weak.

The first great event was called The Year of Withered Fields. A long drought struck Greenmeadow Plains, and crops failed across the land. The Farmer shared his remaining grain with other villages instead of keeping it. Because of that sacrifice, no village starved completely. From that year onward, Greenmeadow became known as the heart that feeds Eldoria.

Soon after, traders traveling through what is now Emberfall Crossing were attacked by raiders. The villages gathered and chose their first united leader. That was when the role of Mayor was officially created. A central council was formed, and Emberfall became the administrative heart of the region.

Years later came The Night of Burning Carts. Merchants were ambushed near Stoneford Outpost, and several trade caravans were lost. The Chief reorganized the guards, built watchtowers, and strengthened the outpost. Since then, Stoneford has stood as the shield of Eldoria.

In the forests near Westwood Hollow, ancient ruins were discovered by an Adventurer who uncovered relics from a forgotten civilization. These ruins revealed that Eldoria was once part of a much larger kingdom that fell centuries ago during an unknown war. The stories brought back from those ruins inspired unity and pride among the villages.

More recently, whispers spread about The Silver Shadow, a mysterious Thief first seen in Windreach Fields. Some say the Thief steals only from the greedy. Others claim no one is safe. Though no one has caught them, their actions have forced villages to strengthen alliances and improve communication.

Because of these events, Eldoria is no longer just a group of settlements. It is a region shaped by hardship, sacrifice, protection, discovery, and mystery. Its people remember the past, and they know that their unity is the reason they still stand strong today.
